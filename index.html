    How much will it cost?
    Plans start at ₦2,000 per month, about the price of a data bundle. Every new brand starts with a free 7-day trial with full access, and founding members keep the ₦2,000 price for life.

    No. BrandsApp is built for your phone, on Nigerian networks. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run your brand. There are no servers, domains or settings to manage.

    Your own live app at yourname.brandsapp.io with a public website, plus any apps you install: a store, bookings, courses, POS, email marketing, loyalty and more. Your data sits in your own private database.

    Yes. Your brand page carries your name, logo and colours from day one, and higher tiers can remove BrandsApp branding completely.
